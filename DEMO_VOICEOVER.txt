Hello guys, let me show you Horizon — a temporal intelligence layer for DreamDEX Event Contracts.

Right now, DreamDEX gives you a number. Will BTC be up or down? But that number alone doesn't tell you the story. Horizon turns that isolated probability into a trajectory of market conviction — moving through time.

Let's walk through it end to end.

We start at Market Explorer. You can see active assets like BTC across multiple horizons — 5 minute, 1 hour, 4 hours. Each card shows the live probability, a trajectory state, confidence score, and reversal risk. So at a glance, you know not just the number, but how conviction is behaving across time.

When something catches your eye, click Analyze. This is the heart of Horizon. The market regime is named plainly — like Bearish Decay — meaning short-term conviction is positive but weakening the further out you look. Evidence explains why, walking through velocity, persistence, and cross-horizon behavior instead of asking you to just trust a label. Map the Next Hour gives you a heuristic forecast — not a guarantee, but a scenario of where conviction might be headed.

From there, the Strategy Composer turns analysis into something actionable. Three risk profiles — Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive — each with its own suggested direction, entry, and size. Pick one, click Execute Trade, and a real order goes straight to DreamDEX on Somnia Shannon testnet. On-chain, verifiable.

Once the trade is placed, the Thesis Tracker kicks in. Every position stores its entry thesis — the market conditions at the exact moment you opened it. Then it compares that snapshot against live data, and tells you: is the thesis strengthening, weakening, or unchanged? This isn't just tracking your position. It's tracking whether your reason for entering still holds true.

Portfolio ties everything together. Open positions, active theses with confidence and reversal risk, and any resolved trades ready to claim on-chain with one click.

Coming next: historical replay, scenario branching, and state transitions — so traders can plan, not just react.

DreamDEX gives you a number. Horizon gives you the trajectory. Trade the trajectory.
