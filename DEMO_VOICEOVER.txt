Hello guys, let me show you Horizon — a temporal intelligence layer for DreamDEX Event Contracts.

Right now, DreamDEX gives you a number. Will BTC be up or down? But that number alone doesn't tell you the story. Horizon turns that isolated probability into a trajectory of market conviction — moving through time.

Here in Market Explorer, you can see BTC across multiple horizons — 5 minute, 1 hour, 4 hours. Each one shows the probability, trajectory state, confidence, and reversal risk. So you see not just the number, but how conviction is moving.

Click Analyze and here's the core. The market regime is named plainly — like Bearish Decay. Evidence explains why — velocity, persistence, cross-horizon behavior. Map the Next Hour shows where conviction might be headed. The Strategy Composer turns analysis into action — Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive — each with suggested direction and size. Pick one, click Execute, real trade on DreamDEX testnet.

Every trade stores its entry thesis — the market conditions when you entered. The Thesis Tracker compares that against live data. Is it strengthening, weakening, or unchanged? That's the feedback loop.

Portfolio shows your open positions, active theses, and anything ready to claim on-chain.
