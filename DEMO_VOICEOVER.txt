DreamDEX gives traders a probability. Will BTC be up or down? But that number alone doesn't tell a story. Horizon is a temporal intelligence layer that turns that isolated probability into a trajectory of market conviction — moving through time.

Here on the Market Explorer, each asset shows real-time probability across multiple horizons — 5 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours — with a trajectory state, confidence score, and reversal risk. Traders can see at a glance whether conviction is accelerating, decaying, or reversing.

The Analyze page is the heart of Horizon. The market regime is named plainly — like Bearish Decay. Evidence explains why — velocity, persistence, cross-horizon behavior. Map the Next Hour shows a heuristic forecast. The Strategy Composer turns analysis into action — Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive — each with suggested direction and size. Click Execute, and a real order goes to DreamDEX on Somnia Shannon testnet.

The Thesis Tracker closes the loop. Every trade stores its entry thesis — the market conditions at the moment it was placed — and compares it continuously against live data. Is the thesis strengthening, weakening, or unchanged? This isn't just position tracking. It's tracking whether your reason for entering still holds.

The Portfolio shows open positions with entry and current status, active theses with confidence and reversal risk, and any resolved trades ready to claim on-chain.

DreamDEX gives traders a number. Horizon gives them the trajectory — how conviction moves through time, why it's moving, and what to do with that information. Horizon — trade the trajectory.
